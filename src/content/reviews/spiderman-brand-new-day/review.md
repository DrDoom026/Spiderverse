## Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Hey, so I watched Spider-Man: Brand New Day, and my God, this is what Spider-Man is supposed to be. Alone, broken, but still a symbol of hope.

This movie perfectly balances action with emotion, and honestly, the emotional aspect hits really hard. The interactions between **MJ and Ned** especially... they truly hit me. It was just amazing. It really felt like what Spider-Man should be.

And this is exactly what Spider-Man is supposed to be RELETABLE. Spider-Man is everyone's favorite because he is THE RELETABLE character. Even though he's going through everything alone, he still manages to make us laugh, cracking jokes in the most serious situations while carrying the responsibility that comes with his power. It's just amazing.

The background score is really good. Not as good as the previous ones, in my opinion, but it lands where it needs to, so I'm not complaining. I just feel like it could've been even better.

The tone of the movie feels so grounded because of the practical effects and the real locations. You can actually feel the vibe of the city, and that makes such a huge difference.

The other characters did an amazing job as well. The interactions between Spidey and **Punisher**, and even with **Bruce Banner**, felt natural and were genuinely enjoyable.

THE HULK!

Finally, after almost 8 years, we get to see our savage Hulk getting furious again. The action sequence between Hulk and Spider-Man is somewhat short, but it's incredibly enjoyable while it lasts.

This movie also did a great job establishing characters for the future. This is what "Marvel is back" is supposed to look like. Introduce characters in standalone movies first, and then bring them together in films like The Avengers. That's where the interactions become the soul of the movie because we've already spent time with those characters individually.

Now let's talk about the ending.

I think this is the first Spider-Man movie that ends without a proper conclusion. They intentionally leave the threads loose to connect them in future projects. But at its core, the ending is about accepting the truth and simply moving on.

Overall, after a very long time, we finally get to see our friendly neighborhood Spider-Man back on the streets of New York, grounded and relatable.

And for the post-credit scene...

That's not just another random Marvel post-credit scene. Even though it seems pretty simple, trust me, I don't think it is. That little glitch at the end could be hinting at an Incursion, or maybe even another Spider-Man from a different universe.

Personally, I think it might be Tobey Maguire's Peter Parker.

We'll find out in the future.
